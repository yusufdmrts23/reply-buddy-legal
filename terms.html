1. What Reply Buddy does

Reply Buddy is an iOS keyboard that suggests a reply to a message, and an app with a Chat tab.

  • The keyboard: you screenshot a conversation, the app reads the text from that screenshot on your device, and an AI service — Anthropic's Claude — writes one reply you can insert. You can ask for another.
  • Chat: you attach a picture of a conversation and say what you want to say, the picture itself is sent to the AI service, which reads it and writes one reply you can copy. You can ask for up to ten changes in the same chat, and add a newer picture to it when the conversation moves on.

Either way, you choose what to send. What each path sends, and what is kept, is set out in the Privacy Policy.

  • Check any fact in a reply. A reply can state things as fact — a time, a place, a price, a name, what someone said. Those statements may be false, incomplete, misleading or out of date. Check them yourself before you send a reply or rely on it.
