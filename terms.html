1. What Reply Buddy does

Reply Buddy is an iOS keyboard that suggests a reply to a message, and an app with a Chat tab.

  • The keyboard: you screenshot a conversation, the app reads the text from that screenshot on your device, and an AI service — Anthropic's Claude — writes one reply you can insert. You can ask for another.
  • Chat: you attach a picture of a conversation and say what you want to say, the picture itself is sent to the AI service, which reads it and writes one reply you can copy. You can ask for up to ten changes to it in the same chat.

Either way, you choose what to send. What each path sends, and what is kept, is set out in the Privacy Policy.
