Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Reply Buddy: AI Keyboard · Effective 24 September 2026

Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer health data law ask for this separate policy, and Connecticut's privacy law treats consumer health data as sensitive data; this policy applies to Connecticut residents in the same way. Reply Buddy is not a health app and never asks about anyone's health. But a conversation you choose to send for a reply can mention health, and when it does, the AI that writes the reply reads it.

What consumer health data we may collect, and why

Only what is in what you send for a reply: the words of a conversation read from a screenshot, a picture you send from the Chat tab, what you type, and your saved writing samples; and, only if you choose to email it to us — for example with the Report button on a reply in Chat — that reply and anything you add to the email. A conversation holds other people's messages as well as yours, and a picture from Chat also shows their names and photos. If any of it reveals the physical or mental health of anyone in it — you, or someone else, such as a friend who mentions a diagnosis — that is consumer health data: for example a condition, symptom or diagnosis, a treatment or medication, a pregnancy or other reproductive or sexual health information, gender-affirming care, or thoughts of self-harm. While writing the reply, the AI also works out, for that request only, whether the conversation involves someone thinking about harming themselves, so that the app never refuses to help then. We use this data only to write the reply you asked for and to keep that reply safe; our AI provider also screens what it receives for misuse of its service, and keeps what its safety systems flag for longer (see below). Apart from an email you choose to send us, it is processed automatically, by software, and no person at Reply Buddy reads it; an email, such as a report of a reply, we read only to answer it.

Where it comes from

From you, when you ask for a reply, or when you email us. Health information about someone else in a conversation reaches us only inside what you choose to send; we never collect it from them, or from anyone else.

What we share, and with whom

The data above is sent through our hosting provider, Supabase, to our AI provider, Anthropic, which writes the reply. Both act as our processors. An email you send us is held by Google, which provides our support mailbox (Gmail), under its own terms. We do not sell consumer health data, share it for advertising, or share it with anyone else. Our server does not store or log it, and neither the self-harm check nor its answer is kept by us; an email you send us is kept only as long as we need to deal with it. Anthropic deletes what it is sent within 30 days, keeps it for up to two years if its safety systems flag it, and longer where the law requires. No other company collects consumer health data about your activity over time or across apps or websites through Reply Buddy.

Your rights, and how to use them

These rights belong to everyone whose consumer health data we collect — including someone whose messages or photos were in a conversation a Reply Buddy user sent, even if they have never used the app. You can ask us to confirm whether we collect, share or sell consumer health data about you, to give you access to it with a list of everyone we have shared it with and how to contact them, to review and correct it, and to delete it; if you use Reply Buddy, you can withdraw consent to AI replies at any time by turning AI replies off in the app's Settings. Email tiko23miko23@gmail.com, with the anonymous ID from Settings → About if you can. We answer within 45 days, and tell you if we need up to 45 more. If we decline, you can appeal by replying to our answer with “Appeal”; we decide an appeal within 45 days and tell you how to contact your state's attorney general if you disagree. Our server keeps none of this data, so there is normally nothing of it on our side to show you, correct or delete; an email you sent us, such as a report of a reply, we delete on request. If you do not use Reply Buddy, we have no identifier for you, and our server kept nothing that could show whether a user ever sent a conversation you were in, so we cannot confirm either way; we will tell you that in writing, with who would have received anything a user sent about you (Supabase and Anthropic, as above) and how to contact them, and we will search our support mailbox for the name, number or address you give us and delete anything about you we find there. What our AI provider still holds is not tied to any Reply Buddy identifier, though a conversation or picture that was sent may show your name or photo; Anthropic does not delete individual requests early, and it is deleted on the schedule above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and we describe a material change at the top of this page and in the release notes of the app update that goes with it before it takes effect.
