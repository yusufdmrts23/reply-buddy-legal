Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Reply Buddy: AI Keyboard · Effective 24 September 2026

Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer health data law ask for this separate policy. Reply Buddy is not a health app and never asks about anyone's health. But a conversation you choose to send for a reply can mention health, and when it does, the AI that writes the reply reads it.

What consumer health data we may collect, and why

Only what is in what you send for a reply: the words of a conversation read from a screenshot, a picture you send from the Chat tab, what you type, and your saved writing samples; and, only if you choose to email it to us — for example with the Report button on a reply in Chat — that reply and anything you add to the email. If any of it mentions someone's physical or mental health, a condition, a treatment, or thoughts of self-harm, that is consumer health data. While writing the reply, the AI also works out, for that request only, whether the conversation involves someone thinking about harming themselves, so that the app never refuses to help then. We use this data only to write the reply you asked for and to keep that reply safe. Apart from an email you choose to send us, it is processed automatically, by software, and no person at Reply Buddy reads it; an email, such as a report of a reply, we read only to answer it.

Where it comes from

From you, when you ask for a reply, or when you email us.

What we share, and with whom

The data above is sent through our hosting provider, Supabase, to our AI provider, Anthropic, which writes the reply. Both act as our processors. An email you send us is held by Google, which provides our support mailbox (Gmail), under its own terms. We do not sell consumer health data, share it for advertising, or share it with anyone else. Our server does not store or log it, and neither the self-harm check nor its answer is kept by us; an email you send us is kept only as long as we need to deal with it. Anthropic deletes what it is sent within 30 days, keeps it for up to two years if its safety systems flag it, and longer where the law requires. No other company collects consumer health data about your activity over time or across apps or websites through Reply Buddy.

Your rights, and how to use them

You can ask us to confirm whether we collect, share or sell consumer health data about you, to give you access to it with a list of everyone we have shared it with and how to contact them, to review and correct it, and to delete it; you can withdraw consent to AI replies at any time by turning AI replies off in the app's Settings. Email tiko23miko23@gmail.com, with the anonymous ID from Settings → About if you can. We answer within 45 days, and tell you if we need up to 45 more. If we decline, you can appeal by replying to our answer with “Appeal”; we decide an appeal within 45 days and tell you how to contact your state's attorney general if you disagree. Our server keeps none of this data, so there is normally nothing of it on our side to show you, correct or delete; an email you sent us, such as a report of a reply, we delete on request. What our AI provider still holds carries no identifier that could match it to you, Anthropic does not delete individual requests early, and it is deleted on the schedule above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and we describe a material change at the top of this page and in the release notes of the app update that goes with it before it takes effect.
