Privacy Policy

Reply Buddy: AI Keyboard · Effective 3 September 2026

Reply Buddy helps you write replies to messages. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your information. It is written to describe the app as it actually behaves, not as a general template.

The short version. Your screenshots never leave your phone. The text of your last three messages is sent to an AI service to write replies, and neither we nor that service keeps it in a permanent record. There is no account, we never see your name or email, and nothing is sent anywhere until you switch AI replies on yourself.

1. Who we are

Reply Buddy is published by Yusuf Demirtaş, an individual developer, who is the data controller for the purposes of this policy. You can reach us at tiko23miko23@gmail.com.

2. There is no account

Reply Buddy does not ask you to register. It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, address, contacts or date of birth. When the app first needs to talk to our server it creates an anonymous identifier — a random string with nothing personal in it — so that your daily allowance and subscription status can be recognised. That identifier is not linked to your identity.

3. Screenshots stay on your phone

Reply Buddy works by reading a screenshot of a conversation. To be precise about what that involves:

  • The app reads only your single most recent screenshot, and only when you open the keyboard or tap the capture button. It does not browse, scan or index your photo library.
  • The text is extracted on your device, using Apple's built-in on-device text recognition.
  • The image itself is never uploaded. It does not reach our servers or any third party. Only the recovered text is sent, and only after you have turned AI replies on.

This requires Photos permission, which iOS asks for during setup. If you decline, the app cannot read screenshots; typing and your saved replies continue to work.

4. What is sent when you ask for replies

When you request replies, the following is sent to our server and then to our AI provider:

SentDetail

Before anything is sent, text that looks like a verification or one-time code is replaced with •••• so it cannot leave your device.

We never send: the screenshot image, your contacts, the name of the app you are replying in, your location, your identity, or anything you type on the keyboard outside of a reply request.

5. Nothing goes anywhere until you agree

The AI features are off until you explicitly turn them on inside the app. Until you do, no message content of any kind is transmitted.

6. What we keep

On our servers

We do not store your message content. Conversation text, drafts and generated replies are not written to our database and are not written to our logs. They exist only for the seconds it takes to produce your replies. What we do store, tied to your anonymous identifier, is: a daily count of how many times you asked for replies, your subscription status, and the totals we need to monitor cost.

On your phone

The most recent conversation the app read is kept in a private container on your device so the keyboard can use it. It is overwritten every time you capture a new one — there is no history, and no earlier conversation is retained. Deleting the app removes it.

7. The AI provider

Replies are generated by Anthropic's Claude models. We want to be exact here rather than reassuring, because the distinction matters:

  • Anthropic's commercial terms state that Anthropic may not train models on customer content from its services.
  • Anthropic's privacy documentation states that inputs and outputs are automatically deleted within 30 days of receipt or generation, and may be kept longer where a usage-policy violation is flagged.

So "we do not store your messages" is true of us, and thirty days is not zero. We will not claim your conversations vanish the instant they are used, because that would not be accurate.

8. Analytics and crash reports

The main app — not the keyboard — sends a small amount of product analytics through PostHog and crash reports through Sentry. What is sent is event names and counts, such as app_opened, full_access_granted, sample_reply_generated and whether a purchase completed.

No message content, no reply text and no draft is ever included, and the keyboard extension contains no analytics software at all.

9. Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, which records what you bought and whether it is active. We never see or receive your payment details — Apple processes payment and does not share your card information with us.

11. Apple's privacy label

In the App Store, Reply Buddy declares that User Content is collected and not linked to your identity. We declare it because Apple defines collection as transmitting data off the device and storing it in readable form for longer than it takes to service the request — and our AI provider's thirty-day deletion window is longer than that. We would rather over-declare than describe this inaccurately.

12. Children

Reply Buddy is not directed at children. It is rated for users aged 17 and over and should not be used by anyone under 13, or under the minimum age of digital consent where you live.

13. Your rights

Because there is no account, most of your data is either on your device or is an anonymous counter. You can delete everything held on your device by deleting the app. To ask what is held against your anonymous identifier, or to have it deleted, email tiko23miko23@gmail.com. Depending on where you live you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability, and the right to complain to your data protection authority.

14. Data transfers and security

Our servers and our AI provider operate in the United States, so if you use the app elsewhere your message text is processed there. All transmission is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). The conversation stored on your device is written with iOS file protection so it is unreadable while your phone is locked.

15. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and material changes will be signalled in the app. Continuing to use Reply Buddy after a change means you accept the updated policy.
