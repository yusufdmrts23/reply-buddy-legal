Reply Buddy: AI Keyboard

Legal documents and support

Reply Buddy is an iOS keyboard that suggests a reply to your messages. You screenshot a conversation, the text is read on your device, and an AI — Anthropic's Claude — writes one reply for you. You can ask for another, and you choose what to send.

The app also has a Chat tab: you attach a picture of a conversation and say what you want to say, and the picture itself is sent to the AI to write the reply, after you allow it. Your chats stay on your phone until you delete them.
