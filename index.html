Reply Buddy: AI Keyboard

Legal documents and support

Reply Buddy is an iOS keyboard that suggests replies to your messages. You screenshot a conversation, the text is read on your device, and reply options are written for you to choose from.

Support

Questions, bug reports, privacy requests: tiko23miko23@gmail.com

To manage or cancel a subscription, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Subscriptions cannot be cancelled from inside Reply Buddy — Apple handles that.
